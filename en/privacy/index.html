What data do we process?

  • What you enter in the contact form: your name, email address, country and your question. The language of the page on which you fill in the form is sent along.
  • What you send us yourself by email or phone.
  • Technical data that every web server receives, such as your IP address and the time of your visit. Our hosting partner uses this only to keep the website secure and working.

Why do we use your data?

  • To answer your question and, if you wish, to schedule an introductory meeting. We do this at your request, with a view to a possible agreement (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • To perform an agreement with you if you become a client (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • To meet legal obligations, such as our accounting (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR).
  • To keep our website secure and prevent misuse of the form (legitimate interest, Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

We do not send newsletters or advertising without your consent. We never sell your data.

Cookies and analytics

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising services. The fonts are also loaded from our own server, not from Google. If this ever changes, we will update this statement and ask for your consent in advance where required.

Who sees your data?

  • Netlify, Inc. hosts the website and receives the messages from the contact form. Netlify is based in the United States. The transfer is based on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.
  • Combell NV (Belgium) provides our email.
  • If you become a client, we share only the data that is needed with partners who help you in Paraguay, such as lawyers, notaries and banks, and only after discussing this with you.

How long do we keep your data?

Messages from people who do not become clients are deleted no later than two years after our last contact. Client data is kept for as long as we work together and afterwards for as long as the law requires, for example seven years for accounting records.

Your rights

You can always ask us to access, correct or delete your data. You can also object to processing, ask us to restrict it, or request your data in a common format. To do so, email info@valtora-alliance.com. We will reply within one month.

If you are not satisfied with how we handle your data, you can lodge a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority, Drukpersstraat 35, 1000 Brussels, www.dataprotectionauthority.be, or with the supervisory authority in your own country.
