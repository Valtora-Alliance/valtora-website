Why diversify beyond Europe

Everything in one place is a risk too.

If your assets, income and home are all in one country, you depend on one government, one currency and one economy. In Belgium, the Netherlands and Europe, the rules are changing fast. Here are the facts, with sources.

Diversifying does not mean escaping taxes. If you live in Belgium or the Netherlands, you remain taxable there on your worldwide income and must report foreign accounts. Paraguay has risks too, such as exchange rates, distance and a different legal system. That is why we help you build a second base legally and transparently, with licensed local partners. This information is not investment advice and reflects the situation in October 2026.

About us

Two entrepreneurs who went first.

Marion was already a regular visitor to Paraguay and saw the opportunities; Andy saw a business in them. We went first ourselves: we invested in real estate there and set up our own company. That is how we got to know the process from the inside, with every step and every pitfall. The opportunities turned out to be too big to keep to ourselves. So we started Valtora Alliance, to guide you the way we would have wanted to be guided: personally, down to earth and with real expertise.
